Digital Transformation & AI-Driven Ecosystem for Indonesia’s Premier Archery Retailer
An end-to-end digital ecosystem scaling a physical archery retailer into an Asia-wide e-commerce platform—featuring AI-accelerated frontend prototyping, interactive scenario testers, serial-number inventory tracking, and integrated B2B dealer loyalty portals.

01 // EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Client & Scope: Pro Archery (Jakarta, Indonesia) × Asian Regional Expansion.

Role & Responsibility: Lead Freelance Product Designer & AI Systems Integrator.

Core Product Suite: B2C E-Commerce Storefront, Customer Warranty Portal, Admin POS & Inventory Management Console, and Corporate Landing Page.

Core Stack: AI Design Tools (Claude, Gemini), VS Code, Antigravity, HTML/CSS Code-Driven Prototyping.

02 // THE STRATEGIC CHALLENGE
Transitioning a brick-and-mortar archery enterprise into a regional e-commerce power required an ultra-fast Proof of Concept (POC) to secure investor/partner alignments while managing complex technical product attributes (custom bow configurations, serial numbers, warranty claims).

Key System & Business Constraints:
Ultra-Fast Execution Needed for POC: Traditional Figma-to-code design cycles were too slow to meet tight partner presentation deadlines.

Complex Inventory Mechanics: Archery gear requires granular tracking—from compound bow serial numbers (SN) to multi-tier dealer pricing (Guest vs. Silver Dealer).

Omnichannel Portal Multiplicity: Designing 4 interconnected portals (B2C Storefront, Customer Portal, Admin POS/Inventory, Company Profile) under a unified design system.

1. AI-Driven Prompt-to-Code Pipeline
Bypassed manual Figma layout creation by utilizing advanced AI prompting (Claude/Gemini) to generate functional frontend code structure directly in VS Code and Antigravity—delivering a live, production-ready landing page POC in just 24 hours.

2. Embedded "Scenario Tester" Demo Widget
Engineered an interactive control widget built directly into the client presentation prototype. Stakeholders could toggle live between User Scenarios (Guest User vs. Silver Dealer Pricing) and Grid Layouts (Default, 4-Column, 5-Column) in real time during review meetings, dramatically reducing layout debate cycles.

3. Granular Order Lifecycle & Serial Number (SN) Tracking
Designed a high-density Admin Portal capable of tracking complex archery inventory—including serial number inputs for high-value bows, warranty claim statuses, order fulfillment timelines, and point-of-sale (POS) integration.

04 // THE DUAL-PORTAL SUITE
A. B2C E-Commerce & Customer Self-Service Portal
Custom Bow & Gear Builder: Interactive product catalog supporting customized archery equipment configurations and integrated payment gateways.

Customer Warranty & Order Tracking: Dedicated portal for users to track order timelines, view serial number certificates, manage loyalty points, and lodge warranty claims.

B. Back-Office Admin & POS Management Console
Inventory & Serial Number Management: Precise tracking of high-tier archery stock requiring individual serial numbers (e.g., Hoyt Altus Compound Bows).

Multi-Tier Member & Discount Rules: Automated pricing engines adjusting storefront rates based on member tiers (Gold Member, Silver Dealer).

Unified POS & Sales Analytics: Integrated Point-of-Sale system synchronizing offline physical store transactions with online e-commerce inventory.

05 // BUSINESS IMPACT & WORKFLOW EFFICIENCY
24-Hour Landing Page Deployment: Leveraged AI-first design workflows to ship a fully functional landing page in 1 day for urgent stakeholder POC presentations.

Streamlined Stakeholder Approvals: The embedded Scenario Tester widget allowed executive decision-makers to test layout variations instantly without waiting for design revision rounds.

Regional Market Readiness: Delivered a complete 4-portal digital infrastructure enabling Pro Archery to validate its business model and expand retail operations across Asian markets.
