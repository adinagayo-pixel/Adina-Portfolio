CASE STUDY: ANLENE HEALTH PASSPORT
[REGION: INDONESIA] · [SCOPE: FIELD SALES ENABLEMENT & HEALTH TECH] · [CLIENT: FONTERRA INDONESIA]

Revamping Field Activation Ecosystems: Medical Data Visualization & Automated Health Passport Delivery for Anlene
A complete product revamp of Fonterra’s field sales tool used by Sales Promotion Girls (SPGs) during event activations—translating complex clinical diagnostic data (Achilles Bone Scanners & Omron Body Composition Scanners) into personalized, actionable health dashboards, automated PDF reports, and WhatsApp/Email messaging pipelines.

01 // EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Client & Brand: Fonterra Indonesia × Anlene (Dairy & Nutrition Enterprise).

Role & Team: Lead UX/UI Designer & Systems Researcher (1-Person Design Team).

Timeline & Governance: August 2023 – October 2023 (3-Month Fast-Paced Sprint).

Core Product Suite: SPG Field Data Entry Mobile/Tablet App + Customer PDF Health Passport Generator + WhatsApp/Email API Integration.

Core Stack: Figma, FigJam, Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator, Graphic Standard Manual (GSM) Guidelines.

02 // THE STRATEGIC CHALLENGE
On-site brand activations required SPGs to quickly capture participant health metrics from hardware devices during busy event setups. The legacy app lacked clear data visualization, resulting in high entry friction for SPGs and confusing health summaries for consumers.

Key System & Domain Constraints:
Medical Hardware & Data Mapping: Translating diagnostic metrics from two distinct physical devices—GE Lunar Achilles Ultrasonometer (T-Score / Stiffness Index for bone density) and Omron Karada Scan HBF-375 (BMI, Body Fat %, Visceral Fat, Skeletal Muscle %, Body Age)—into standard visual ranges.

High-Stress Field Sales Workflow: SPGs needed offline draft-saving capabilities, rapid data editing, and instant status tracking (Uploaded, Pending) while managing long queues at event booths.

Strict Brand Compliance (Fonterra GSM): Applying Anlene’s Graphic Standard Manual guidelines across mobile interfaces, PDF reports, and activation marketing collateral.

1. Physical Device & Field Observational Research
Analyzed operational documentation, user manuals, and field video recordings of SPG booth interactions to map hardware printout outputs directly into digital input fields—ensuring fields mirrored the exact order of physical device readouts.

2. Medical Metric Threshold Categorization
Structured a unified 4-tier color system based on health parameters:

Blue (Low / Below Average)

Green (Normal / Ideal State)

Yellow (High / Moderate Risk)

Red (Very High / High Osteoporosis Risk)

3. Dual-Journey Alignment (SPG Efficiency vs. Customer Value)
Re-engineered the journey so SPGs could capture survey preferences (Anlene consumption history, mobility status) and medical inputs in under 2 minutes, while customers immediately received a QR code or direct WhatsApp/Email link to their personalized report.

04 // SYSTEM DELIVERABLES & MODULE BREAKDOWN
A. SPG Activation & Data Entry Console (Tablet/Mobile)
Rapid Multi-Step Intake: Sequential forms for participant registration, milk consumption surveys, Achilles bone scan inputs, and Omron body composition fields.

Draft Saving & Event Upload Status: A centralized dashboard listing participant records, upload statuses (Uploaded, Pending), and instant edit capabilities to prevent data loss during network drops.

B. Customer Health Passport & Recommendation Dashboard
Visual Metric Gauges: Intuitive visual indicators comparing participant results against ideal age-adjusted health baselines (e.g., BMI semi-circle gauge, T-Score bone density indicator).

Targeted Product & Lifestyle Recommendations: Automated recommendation engines suggesting specific Anlene milk variants and daily active movement tips tailored to individual health scores.

Automated PDF & WhatsApp Dispatch: One-click PDF report generator enabling SPGs or automated triggers to dispatch full health passports via WhatsApp Official API or Email.

05 // BUSINESS IMPACT & KEY DELIVERABLES
Streamlined Field Operations: Reduced SPG data input friction through mapped form structures and draft-saving features during high-volume event activations.

Empowered Field Sales Conversion: Provided SPGs with clear visual argument points (e.g., highlighting low T-Scores or high body age) to recommend specific Anlene product lines at the point of sale.

Flawless Brand Governance: Successfully unified Anlene’s digital app interface, PDF health reports, and physical event marketing materials under Fonterra’s strict Graphic Standard Manual guidelines.
