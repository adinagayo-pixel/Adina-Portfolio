Gamified Financial Evaluation & Interactive Acquisition Campaign for GEGI Singapore
A rapid-sprint interactive campaign tool engineered for Great Eastern General Insurance (GEGI) Singapore—combining scroll-based gamified evaluation, localized Singlish persona mechanics, Adobe Experience Manager (AEM) integration, and organic social-sharing loops under a strict 2-week execution timeline.

01 // EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Client & Market: Great Eastern General Insurance Ltd (GEGI) / Singapore.

Role & Responsibility: Lead UI/UX Designer (Dedicated Outsourcing).

Execution Sprint: 2 Weeks Rapid Sprint (March 2026).

Core Stack: Figma, Adobe Experience Manager (AEM), Google Analytics Event Schema, Custom SVG Asset Engineering.

02 // THE STRATEGIC CHALLENGE
Great Eastern Singapore required an engaging digital marketing tool to assess critical illness (CI) protection gaps among prospective Singaporean clients while capturing qualified lead sign-ups.

Key System & Business Constraints:
Ultra-Tight Timeline (2-Week Sprint): High-stakes execution to secure GEGI’s first interactive campaign tender in the Singapore market.

Enterprise Technical Integration: Must seamlessly embed within Adobe Experience Manager (AEM) with precise Google Analytics tracking events tagged on every question and scroll interaction.

UX & Content Density Challenge: Designing a continuous scroll-based evaluation experience for a 7-question matrix without relying on traditional "Next" buttons, avoiding user fatigue on high-density pages.

1. Scroll-Driven UX Matrix
Eliminated traditional multi-step wizard buttons in favor of an intuitive, scroll-based question flow. Users navigate financial questions (income ranges, sum insured expectations) alongside visual preference cards (hobbies, food choices) in one continuous, fluid layout.

2. Localized Character Personas & Culture Alignment
Collaborated with a dedicated illustrator to establish brand-compliant, Singapore-centric character personas representing different CI Gap tiers (e.g., Steady Pom Pi Pi, Agak-Agak Planner, Hopeful Thinker, YOLO Warrior). Fine-tuned visual assets and localized Singlish terminology based on client feedback to ensure authentic market resonance.

3. Unprompted Growth Initiative (Instagram Story Assets)
Proactively designed customized Instagram Story shareable cards for each persona result—going beyond the initial brief to drive organic, word-of-mouth brand awareness across social channels.

04 // DESIGN PROCESS & WORKFLOW PIPELINE
[METHODOLOGY: RAPID SPRINT & GAMIFIED UX] · [TECH STACK: AEM & GA TRACKING]

Phase 1: Alignment & Asset Direction
Brand & Visual Guidelines Parsing: Analyzed GEGI Singapore’s corporate branding rules and illustration style to select appropriate visual assets and primary color themes.

Illustration Collaboration: Coordinated with a graphic illustrator to produce custom SVG character assets tailored specifically for the 4 financial persona outcomes.

Phase 2: Interaction Design & Prototyping
Continuous Scroll Layout: Built responsive mobile and desktop layouts optimized for vertical scroll interactions, balancing content density with visual breathing space.

Interactive Prototyping: Created rapid Figma prototypes to demonstrate scroll transitions and result-calculation states for Singapore executive approval.

Phase 3: Localization & Analytics Tagging
Cultural Copywriting Refinement: Incorporated feedback from the Singapore client team to align localized terms (such as validating the usage of "Pom Pi Pi" for the high-readiness tier).

Analytics & Developer Handoff: Prepared production-ready SVG assets and mapped Google Analytics event triggers across every questionnaire selection point for seamless AEM developer implementation.

05 // BUSINESS IMPACT & KEY DELIVERABLES
Won First Singapore Market Tender: Successful rapid-sprint delivery led to the acquisition of GEGI’s inaugural interactive campaign tender in Singapore, receiving direct commendations from regional management.

First-Round Live Deployment: Completed design, stakeholder review, developer asset preparation, and AEM integration within the 2-week window.

Organic Shareability: The unprompted Instagram Story sharing design successfully expanded campaign reach beyond paid channels into user-generated social loops.

Foundation for Regional Gamification: Established a reusable interactive campaign template for GEGI’s future user acquisition initiatives.
