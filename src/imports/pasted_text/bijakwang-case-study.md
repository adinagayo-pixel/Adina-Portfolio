Massive Live Multiplayer Gamification & AI-Audio Integrated Financial Literacy Platform for mySalam Malaysia
An end-to-end interactive gamified competition platform engineered for mySalam Malaysia—supporting up to 100,000 concurrent live tournament players, Kahoot-inspired team invite mechanics, real-time leaderboard calculations, and Gemini AI-synthesized contextual game audio.

01 // EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Client & Initiative: mySalam Malaysia × myKawan (Kementerian Digital & ASEAN 2025 Financial Literacy Initiative).

Role & Scope: Lead UI/UX Designer & Gamification Architect.

Target Audience & Scale: University Students (Varsiti Terengganu, Penang, etc.) and General Malaysian Public (Targeting 100,000 concurrent live participants for World & Malaysia Book of Records attempts).

Core Product Suite: Daily Self-Practice Portal + Live Multiplayer Tournament Engine + Team Management System.

Core Stack: Figma, Kahoot Interaction Benchmarking, Gemini AI Audio Generation (Contextual Game Sounds), TikTok Live Stream Integration.

02 // THE STRATEGIC CHALLENGE
mySalam needed to transform financial education into a viral, high-stakes competition across Malaysian universities. The system required a dual-phase architecture supporting both self-paced daily learning and ultra-low-latency live broadcast events.

Key System & Interaction Constraints:
High-Concurrency Live Multiplayer Execution: Managing real-time synchronized question distribution and answer locking across thousands of simultaneous live stream players (via TikTok Live / On-site Hosts).

Flexible Team Matchmaking (Individual vs. 3-Player Teams): Designing seamless invitation loops (unique team codes, email invites, shareable links) so users could compete individually or form 3-person squads.

Audience Retention & Audio Immersion: Enhancing game feedback through contextual soundscapes (lobby waiting rooms, countdown timers, speed bonuses, live leaderboard updates) without heavy media assets.

1. Squad Formation & Team Invitation Engine
Engineered an intuitive modal workflow allowing team captains to set squad names, generate 8-character join codes (KOD-1234), search player emails, and track pending invitation statuses before locking squad rosters.

2. Kahoot-Style Speed & Accuracy Scoring Engine
Implemented a synchronized answer-locking interface awarding base points for correct choices (+100 pts) alongside dynamic speed bonuses (+15 pts for rapid responses). The UI clearly displays real-time answer distribution charts after each question phase.

3. Contextual Gemini AI Audio Integration
Utilized Gemini AI music generation tools to compose lightweight, custom instrumental sound effects tailored to game states: relaxed ambient music in the waiting lobby, high-tension countdown tracks during answer submission, and triumphant celebratory audio for leaderboard podium reveals.

04 // SYSTEM DELIVERABLES & MODULE BREAKDOWN
A. Daily Self-Practice & Training Hub
Daily Financial Quizzes: Self-paced modules covering budgeting rules (e.g., 50/30/20 rule), savings strategies, and insurance literacy with streak trackers.

Solo Practice Leaderboards: Localized rankings encouraging consistent engagement prior to live tournament dates.

B. Live Multiplayer Tournament Engine
Lobby & Matchmaking Console: Interactive waiting rooms displaying live participant counts (e.g., 100 out of 100,000 players ready) and host broadcast status.

Synchronized Question & Option Display: High-contrast option cards with timer indicators and an instant [Lock My Answer] confirmation trigger.

Live Leaderboard & Podium Visualizer: Real-time ranking displays showing top team scores, point breakdowns, and overall tournament progression.

05 // BUSINESS IMPACT & KEY DELIVERABLES
National Record-Breaking Scale: Successfully delivered the gamified platform infrastructure powering official national financial literacy roadshows (including Varsiti Terengganu and Karnival Celik Kewangan Penang).

Multi-Format Engagement: Supported both individual competitors and 3-player university teams across daily practice and live broadcast events.

AI-First Audio Production: Leveraged Gemini AI to produce a complete sound system independently, eliminating external audio licensing costs while elevating player immersion.
